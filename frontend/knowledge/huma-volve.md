# Huma-Volve 🚀

## What is Huma-Volve?

**Huma-Volve** is not a bootcamp.

It's a **simulated real-world work experience program** — a place where junior developers stop learning *about* software and start *living* it.

Students join structured teams, work on real projects, and are guided by an industry senior who does what real tech leads do: reviews code, shares hard-earned knowledge, challenges assumptions, and transfers the kind of wisdom that only comes from years in the field.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Most learning programs teach you how to write code. Very few teach you how to work.

> "I know React, but I've never shipped a feature with a team."
> "I can build CRUD apps, but I don't know how to handle a code review."
> "I studied for months, but I still feel like I don't belong in a real company."

Huma-Volve closes the gap between **knowing** and **doing** — between the student mindset and the professional one.

---

## How It Works

### 🏢 Join a Team, Not a Class
Students are placed into cross-functional teams — just like real software companies. You're not a student here. You're a junior developer with responsibilities.

### 🧑‍💻 Work on Real Projects
Teams tackle real software challenges: building features, debugging production-like issues, writing documentation, and making architectural decisions.

### 👨‍🏫 Guided by an Industry Senior
Every team is mentored by an experienced engineer who:
- Conducts **code reviews** (not rubber-stamping — real, constructive feedback)
- Shares **how software companies actually operate**
- Teaches professional norms: PR etiquette, sprint culture, technical communication
- Acts as the team lead, not a classroom instructor

### 🔁 Learning Through Doing
There are no lectures. Knowledge is transferred *in context* — when you're in the middle of a problem, when it matters most.

## Who Is This For?

Huma-Volve is designed for:

- Junior developers who finished a bootcamp or a degree and feel **stuck**
- Self-taught developers who know the syntax but not the **workflow**
- Career switchers who want to build real experience **before** their first job
- Anyone who's been rejected at interviews not for lacking skills — but for lacking **context**

---

## Our Philosophy

We believe the most valuable things in software can't be taught in a course:

- How to give and receive feedback without ego
- How to disagree with a technical decision professionally
- How to ask for help without feeling like you're failing
- How to write code that other humans can read and maintain

These are **human skills**. They live at the intersection of technical craft and professional maturity.

That's why we're called **Huma-Volve** — human evolution, through real experience.
